Thank you · Metagame 2026

Metagame 2026 is wrapped!

Thank you all for coming and building and playing together this past weekend. Metagame is a passion project co-built by the team, our speakers, and most of all our attendees, and we are grateful to all of those people for showing up and making Metagame 2026 a success. We are so, so looking forward to seeing you again next year! To that point…

Next year

Tickets to Metagame 2027 are officially live! Even earlier than early bird, Egg Tier tickets for next year’s Metagame are on sale this week only, through 11/15, for just $150 (or pay what you want above that).

While this may be a fantastic deal, we also want Metagame to be as accessible as possible, and have a goal of making this price available for everyone. If you want to help make that happen, you can support us by joining the team to help make the con happen, reaching out about sponsoring, or electing to chip in more on your Egg Tier ticket. Connecting us to potential sponsors who are not you is also very encouraged.

Get an Egg Tier ticket

Feedback

Metagame is constantly aiming to improve. We’d love all attendees to share feedback about their experiences at the con. You can give us general feedback, and you can give feedback about specific sessions or activities you participated in.

Get involved!

The Metagame team would love more collaborators to make next year run smoothly. If you’re interested in helping out, as a volunteer, team member, or otherwise, please fill out our interest form and we will reach out.

Postmortem meeting

Everyone on the Metagame team has a ton of thoughts and lessons to integrate from this conference, and we want everyone who attended to be a part of that. To that end, we want to invite you to our postmortem meeting tonight at 7 PM PST, where you can hear some of the team’s thoughts and discuss and share feedback of your own.

Join the postmortem

Lost, please find

We were so happy to have our friends at Alleycat join us for this weekend. They are unfortunately missing a number of their PDN devices, which some of you may have used at Metagame. If you know of an obscure place you may have left one of these, or mistakenly took it home with you, please reach out to them at @cyonic on Discord, or at elli@failfactory.io.

If you lost or found anything at Metagame, you can go to our #lost-found channel to try your luck.
