November 6–8 · Lighthaven, Berkeley

See you this weekend!

Hello! The Metagame team has been hard at work at Lighthaven all week, setting up and preparing, and we are very much looking forward to seeing you. Metagame starts today, Friday, November 6, at Lighthaven, 2740 Telegraph Ave, Berkeley. Doors open at 2 PM.

Getting in

To get in, you need to have claimed your ticket. Make an account on the Metagame website and enter the ticket code that came with your purchase, then hit Claim. If you make your account with the email you used at checkout, the code fills in on its own. Your claimed ticket is what we check at the door, so please do this before you arrive.

If you bought a ticket for someone else, that same ticket code is how they claim it: send it to them and they enter it from their own account. If someone bought your ticket for you, ask them for the code.

The schedule

The full weekend schedule is on the website. RSVP and bookmark any session you want to flag for later. Some sessions have limited spots or need advance sign-ups, so if there is something you don’t want to miss, RSVP to it now.

The venue

A few things to know about Lighthaven:

Wi-Fi: the network is LightFi and the password is wearethelight.

Parking nearby is very limited, and please don’t park on the surrounding two blocks. Lighthaven’s neighbors notice.

No smoking anywhere on campus.

Food: free snacks and paid food trucks are available on campus.

Some areas of campus are off limits. Please quest and puzzle responsibly and respect any out-of-bounds signage.

A cat lives on campus and is not allowed inside. If you see him heading for a door, don’t let him in, and if you spot him inside a building, shoo him out or let someone know.

A couple more things

Lodging: we added some last-minute options if you want to stay at or near the venue. Book a room.

The Night Market is Friday from 7 to 10 PM and is free and open to the public, so bring friends who don’t have a ticket.

Kids under 13 attend free, and childcare is available for ages 5–12. If you are bringing children, please register them so we know to expect them.

Questions? Reply to this email, or ask in #ask-staff on the Metagame Discord. We are going to be very busy this weekend, but we will get back to you as soon as we can. See you soon!
