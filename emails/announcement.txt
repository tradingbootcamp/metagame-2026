METAGAME 2026
Nov 6-8 | Lighthaven Campus (https://lighthaven.space), Berkeley, CA

We're back! Metagame is a weekend conference devoted to games, in the
broadest sense of the word: any experience designed to be played or actively
consumed. If building it involves asking the question "what would the person
experiencing this choose to do next?" we want it at Metagame.

GET TICKETS
https://metagame.games/#tickets

$325 early bird (regular $425). Early bird price ends September 30th.

Brought to you by Rogue Signal, Francisco San, Manifund, and Outside the Asylum.

THE MEGAGAME
-----------------------------------------------------------

The Metagame Megagame is an immersive puzzle hunt interwoven into the smaller
games and sessions of the conference. If you want your session, game, or puzzle
to play into the overarching structure, let us know. Some themes we're
particularly interested in: alliances, maps, alchemy, space travel, gems,
and/or stars.

Contribute to the Megagame:
https://airtable.com/appROpPV6XUP4CSqX/pagMOssJPZrZU6YLP/form
Due October 10th.
