Metagame is less than a week away!

We have a handful of updates for you, some more important than others. Please give each section a look in case it applies to you.

Claim your ticket

You can now make an account on the Metagame website, where you can view and manage your ticket, RSVP to sessions, and more. Your claimed ticket is your admission, so please claim it before you arrive.

To claim it, hit Claim on your account’s ticket page and enter the code from your purchase email. If you sign up with the same email you used at checkout, the code should fill itself in.

Claim your ticket

The schedule is up

The schedule is live (and still very much in flux). Some sessions are smaller and high-commitment, like a TTRPG session where a set group is expected to stay through to the end. If one of those catches your eye, grab a spot while they last.

See the schedule →

Bring your friends

Once you’ve claimed your ticket, hit Refer on your ticket page to get a referral code. A friend who uses it gets our Family & Friends pack rate, and you’ll both get special friendship-themed swag at the con.
