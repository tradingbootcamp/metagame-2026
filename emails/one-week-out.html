Metagame is less than a week away!

We are less than a week out, and are super excited to see you at Lighthaven! Before then we have a few important announcements to make sure you are ready for an action packed weekend. Please read each section carefully in case it applies to you.

Claim your ticket

You can now make an account on the Metagame website! This will allow you to view and manage your ticket, RSVP to sessions, and more.

You can Claim your ticket using the code that was sent with your purchase email, which should prefill if you make your Metagame account with the email you used at checkout. Your claimed ticket is your admission, so please claim it before you arrive.

Claim your ticket

The schedule is up

You can see the current (still rather in flux) schedule at the link below. RSVPs for sessions are open now! Some sessions have limited slots or require advance signups (think LARPs or TTRPGs that need a specific number of players).

See the schedule

Bring your friends

Bring your friends to Metagame! On the ticket page, once you’ve claimed your ticket, you can copy an invite link to give to your friends. If a friend uses this link to buy a standard ticket they’ll get the same discount as our Family & Friends pack rate, and if they buy any type of ticket you will both receive special friendship-themed swag at the con.

Sessions to sign up for now

The Ghost Games is a game shaped by the community’s votes on different questions. We would love as many people as possible to vote in this survey, even if you aren’t planning on attending the session.

Vote in the survey

We have a small number of sessions that need people to sign up or apply well in advance, either because there is material players should familiarize themselves with beforehand, or because they have an extremely small number of players. If any of these interest you, sign up now:

Slaydium Games: An Unforgettable D&D Gladiatorial Experience

Sat, Nov 7 · 5:00–6:55 PM PT
Eigen Hall · Building E, 1st floor

D&D becomes a spectator sport! Watch gladiatorial games unfold in one of twenty deadly arenas, with level 10 characters in a fight to the death.

A live-play game for 4–5 players in front of an audience. If you have experience with D&D, are comfortable on a stage, and would like to enter the Slaydium, reach out to Casey directly at casey@slaydiumgames.com.

 

Thumper Braxton’s Emergency Birthday Wish Vote

Sat, Nov 7 · 7:00–8:55 PM PT
Bayes Attic · Building B, top floor

Thumper’s 13th birthday is in an hour, and the voices in his head must pick his wish. A comedic LARP: Dr. Strangelove meets Inside Out.

This LARP has material, including a questionnaire, that will be sent out in advance to participants who have claimed a spot. Claim a spot here.

 

The Treacherous Turn: Oneshot Special

Sun, Nov 8 · 7:00–9:55 PM PT
Eigen Hall · Building E, 1st floor

A research-supported TTRPG where players collectively scheme as a misaligned AI, in a bespoke oneshot run by designer iris lemony.

An RSVP means you’ll spectate this live-play session. If you’re interested in being one of the players onstage, apply here.

 

The Good People of Sheepfield: A LARP

Sun, Nov 8 · 3:00–5:55 PM PT
Thingspace · Building A, 2nd floor

A secrets-and-powers LARP in a seemingly placid 19th-century Welsh village, where tensions and secrets simmer beneath the surface.

This one needs exactly 11 players, so if you’re interested, claim a spot. There’s general information and an optional casting survey, if you’d like a character assigned early.

 

Worlds To Come: The Hebrew roleplaying game

Sun, Nov 8 · 11:00 AM–1:55 PM PT
Bayes Attic · Building B, top floor

Play miracle workers who speak truths in the language of Creation to rewrite people’s guiding words, with designer Lexi Kohanski.

Up to 6 players. Interested players, please fill out the application survey.
