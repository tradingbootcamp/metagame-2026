Metagame is less than a week away!

Metagame is coming up in under one week! We have various variably important bits of information to share with you. Please check each section carefully in case it applies to you.

Claim your ticket

You can now make an account on the Metagame website! This will allow you to view and manage your ticket, RSVP to sessions, and more.

You can Claim your ticket using the code that was sent with your purchase email, which should prefill if you make your Metagame account with the email you used at checkout. Your claimed ticket is your admission, so please claim it before you arrive.

Claim your ticket

The schedule is up

You can see the current (still rather in flux) schedule below. Note: some of the sessions are more limited and high-commitment (think a TTRPG session that a set number of people are expected to stay through). Grab spots at those you’re interested in committing to!

Bring your friends

Bring your friends to Metagame! On the ticket page, once you’ve claimed your ticket, you can click the Refer button to get a referral code. If a friend uses it, they’ll get the same discount as our Family & Friends pack rate, and both of you will receive special friendship-themed swag at the con.
