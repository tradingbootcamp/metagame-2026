Volunteers, Family Packs, and Low Income Tickets

Metagame 2026 is coming to Berkeley next weekend! It’s a conference dedicated to games of every kind: three days of puzzles, board games, talks, workshops, a Megagame puzzle hunt, and more, November 6–8 at Lighthaven.

We want you to attend. We’d also love your help making it happen.

We’re looking for volunteers to help before and during the con: welcoming people at registration, checking rooms, setting up sessions, coordinating speakers, spreading the word, and more. If you’d like to be part of the crew, apply today!
