Metagame is almost here. Join us November 6–8 at Lighthaven in Berkeley.
 

November 6–8 · Lighthaven, Berkeley

Have you gotten
your ticket yet?

Last year was a blast, and we’re aiming even higher for Metagame 2026. We’re putting together a packed weekend of games, puzzles, and wonderfully unusual things to play. We can’t wait to show you what’s in store.

Bring a game of your own, find people to play with, or just come ready to try something new.

A few of the confirmed sessions

  • Dungeon Court with Randy Lubin and Raph D’Amico: plead your fantastical case in a one-minute courtroom showdown.
  • Jadefall with Chris Kay and Devin Lehmacher: uncover treasure-holders and hidden traitors in a Chinese Zodiac social-deduction game.
  • The Treacherous Turn: Oneshot Special with Aemilia Dixon and iris lemony: scheme together as a misaligned AI in a bespoke TTRPG adventure.
  • Building a Roleplaying Theme Park with Caro Murphy: brainstorm and pitch your own playable worlds.
  • Spencer’s Secret Dungeon Talk Show with Spencer Beebe: watch guests fight, solve puzzles, and roleplay through a video-game dungeon.

And that’s just a taste of what’s waiting for you.

Join us November 6–8 at Lighthaven in Berkeley. Metagame is almost here. If you’ve been meaning to get your ticket, make a plan to join us.
