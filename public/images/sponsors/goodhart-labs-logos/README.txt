Goodhart Labs logo package
goodhartlabs.com

WHAT'S HERE
  stacked      Mark above the name. The primary logo (it's what the website uses).
  horizontal   Mark beside the name. For banners, badges and wide spaces.
  mark         The three-block mark on its own. For small spaces and patterns.
  wordmark     The name on its own, for when the mark already appears nearby.
  avatar       The mark centred on a square, for profile pictures and event listings.

Every logo comes in black (#111111) and white (#FFFFFF), for light and dark backgrounds.

WHICH FILE TO USE
  svg/   Vector. Best for websites and anything a designer will place.
  pdf/   Vector. Best for printers: signage, banners, lanyards, slides.
  png/   Transparent images. "-1600w" for screens, "-6400w" for large print.
  avatar/ Square PNGs (512 and 1024 px) and SVGs, with backgrounds.

Text is converted to outlines, so nothing depends on fonts being installed.

PLEASE
  - Leave clear space around the logo at least as wide as one of the mark's blocks.
  - Use black or white only. Don't recolour the logo, add effects, or stretch it.
  - Don't rebuild the mark from the ░▒▓ characters; the drawn mark is spaced evenly.
  - Keep the mark at least 16 px (5 mm) tall, and the stacked logo at least 40 px (12 mm) tall.
