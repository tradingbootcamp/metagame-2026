# Metagame 2026 brand kit

A working collection derived from the public Metagame website, reviewed September 28, 2026. Sponsor roster updated October 23, 2026. Open `reference/Metagame_2026_Brand_Guide.pdf` for usage guidance.

## Contents

- `logos/`: transparent four-die wordmark and compact single die. Keep their proportions and original colors.
- `palette.json` and `palette.css`: exact values from the website styles. The logo artwork has its own baked-in blue and orange; do not recolor it to the UI accents.
- `photography/`: event imagery used on the site. Check subject and photographer permissions before outside distribution; see the site's [Attributions](https://metagame.games/credits) page.
- `sponsors/`: current website sponsor marks, in their supplied colors. Use them only for Metagame sponsor recognition and confirm roster/tier before publishing.
- `templates/`: editable SVG starting points for a square social post and landscape event card. Open in Figma, Illustrator, or Inkscape. Linked image paths work while files stay in this kit folder; replace copy and imagery as needed.

- Silver: d_model.

- d_model artwork: `sponsors/d-model.png`, copied unchanged from `public/images/sponsors/dmodel_logo.png` in the website repository. Sponsor link: https://dmodel.ai. Silver sponsorship confirmed in the October 11–13 email thread; this is the logo already used on the website. No newer logo was found in the connected sponsor email thread as of October 15, 2026.
- Goodhart Labs artwork: `sponsors/goodhart-labs-black.svg`, `sponsors/goodhart-labs-white.svg`, and matching transparent `.png` files. Copied unchanged from the approved stacked SVG and 1600-pixel PNG artwork in `public/images/sponsors/goodhart-labs-logos/`. Sponsor link: https://goodhartlabs.com. Confirmed Gold sponsor. Use black on light backgrounds and white on dark backgrounds; leave clear space at least one mark block wide and keep the stacked logo at least 40 px (12 mm) tall. - Preserve each sponsor mark's proportions and supplied colors. Use comparable visual weight within a tier, with Gold larger than Silver and Silver larger than Patron.

## Type

- Bebas Neue: display type — https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Bebas+Neue
- Space Grotesk: headings and expressive body — https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Grotesk
- Inter: UI/body — https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter
- Space Mono: labels, dates, metadata — https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Mono
- Roboto: site button labels — https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Roboto

The fonts are referenced, not bundled. Install or load them before editing templates.
