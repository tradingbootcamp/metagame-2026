# Walrusfi — Silver sponsor

Walrusfi (Walrus Security) is a Silver-tier Metagame 2026 sponsor, as confirmed
by David. Logos link to <https://walrusfi.com>.

The three SVG files (`logo-nav.svg`, `logo-walrus-color.svg`, and
`logo-walrus-white.svg`) are Isaac-supplied originals, preserved without changes.
`walrusfi.png` is a transparent PNG derivative of `logo-nav.svg` for email and
other uses that require raster artwork.

Source assets and the PNG derivative are also available in the
[Walrusfi Drive brand-kit folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1ZTcZhiOYsZCNbIZBmWeg6JXUfYUENVGA).

`logo-nav.svg` is also copied to `public/images/sponsors/walrusfi_horiz.svg` for
the home page sponsors section (`src/v2/data/sponsors.ts`).
