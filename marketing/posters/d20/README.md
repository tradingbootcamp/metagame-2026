## Printing

Download the PDF matching your paper. Select that paper size, portrait orientation, actual size / 100%, and **borderless / edge-to-edge** in the printer settings. Disable headers and footers if offered.

The artwork fills the PDF page exactly, without added margins, cropping, or stretching. The PDF requests no print scaling (`PrintScaling=None`), but cannot enable a printer's borderless mode. A printer that cannot print edge-to-edge will leave a margin or clip the edges at 100%; use a borderless-capable printer or print shop for the intended result. Do not use Fit to printable area when printing borderless, since that adds margins.

Both originals have 192 pixels per inch at their intended sizes and are embedded at original resolution. The large artwork is 13 x 19, not 13 x 11. No extra bleed or crop marks are added. These files preserve the supplied QR artwork; no tracking destination was changed. A physical print/phone scan is still needed before a large print run.
